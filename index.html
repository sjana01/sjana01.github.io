Research

Algebraic Topology

My PhD research focused on algebraic topology, especially cohomological methods related to flag manifolds, unordered flag varieties, and group actions. This work develops structural tools for understanding cohomology rings and related algebraic invariants.

  • Cohomology of complete unordered flag manifolds.
  • Mod-2 cohomology rings of alternating subgroups of Coxeter groups of Type B.
  • Connections between group cohomology, topology, and combinatorial algebra.
  • Publications in Transactions of the AMS, Topology and its Applications, and Homology, Homotopy and Applications.

Quantum Computing

As a postdoctoral researcher, I study quantum nonlocality, contextuality, and magic state distillation through algebraic, graph-theoretic, and computational techniques.

  • Developed graph-based frameworks for modeling quantum nonlocality as constraint satisfaction problems.
  • Evaluated 1,000+ instances and improved paradox detection efficiency by roughly 40% using odd-cycle inference.
  • Developed invariant-theoretic methods for magic state distillation, scaling analysis to codes up to 71 qubits / 41 qutrits.
  • Identified high-threshold protocols with approximately 32%–34% noise tolerance.
